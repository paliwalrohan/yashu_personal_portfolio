Executive summary

Zumper is an apartment rental platform that makes it easier for tenants to discover and lease houses, rooms, condos, and apartments. Its mission is to change the way you rent, forever: easier, faster, and more human. Zumper supports tenants through the whole process, from browsing and touring to applying and signing the lease. The company is headquartered in San Francisco, has 200 employees across the U.S., and acquired PadMapper in 2016.

The company offers three types of listings:

  • Long-term (6 months or more)
  • Short-term rentals (1–6 months)
  • Vacation rentals (less than 30 days)

Zumper is the fastest-growing and third-largest rental platform in North America, serving one in three U.S. adults and attracting 175 million visits a year, with 6 million average monthly users. In this report we explored options to support future growth. Because the company’s strength is making renting easier for hosts and tenants, we looked at features useful to every prospective tenant to strengthen that advantage. We also proposed using the platform’s ease of use to target first-time landlords, who are more likely to struggle to rent out their homes, and raising awareness to win share from Airbnb and VRBO in short-term and vacation rentals. Finally, we laid out a roadmap for executing these changes.
