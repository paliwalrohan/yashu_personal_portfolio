When
March 2023
Skills

Read the full report

Problem statement

Increase U.S. sales of CRED, a debt payment and rewards app, through demonstration of value, persuasion techniques, and market analysis.

What I learned

  1. The stages of the sales process, how to identify potential customers, and how to qualify leads.
  2. Sales techniques to persuade customers, such as creating urgency, addressing objections, and building trust.
  3. How to build strong customer relationships, understand needs and preferences, and communicate effectively.
  4. How to close a sale and secure commitments.
  5. How to track sales metrics and analyze data to find improvements and measure what works.
  6. How to manage a sales team, set targets, and motivate and reward the team.
